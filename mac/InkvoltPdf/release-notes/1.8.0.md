Inkvolt PDF speaks Spanish, prints pages sharp instead of as pictures, and keeps working on files made to freeze it. Scanning gets presets and leaves out blank pages, and Snapshot copies part of a page as a picture.

- Inkvolt PDF speaks Spanish: menus, windows, messages, reports and What's New follow the language you choose for it in macOS (System Settings › General › Language & Region › Applications) or in Settings › Language.
- Printing sends pages as they are, not as pictures: text and lines stay sharp at any size, and print jobs are many times smaller and faster to send. A long scan is ready to print in about a second.
- Printing to a printer Inkvolt Print knows shows its state and toner in the print panel, and warns in red when it's off or has a problem such as a paper jam, before you press Print. File › Printer Settings opens Inkvolt Print.
- Scan lists the scanners Inkvolt Print knows straight away; Save as Preset… keeps your scan choices under a name; blank pages are left out (on by default), and the message says how many.
- Snapshot (Tools › Snapshot, ⌥⌘C): drag over part of a page to copy it as a picture, sharp enough to paste into a document or an email.
- Make Accessible reads the bitmap fonts of older TeX papers, tags each heading and paragraph inside a drawing placed on the page, and reads a table cell that spans rows as one cell. The Reading Order pane can change what a piece is (heading, paragraph, caption, footnote, formula, picture).
- Batch's Recognize Text reads scans as Tools › Recognize Text does and keeps the words it wasn't sure of for review; exports from scanned pages say how many suspect words there were.
- A page built to take minutes to read no longer freezes the app: after a few seconds it says so, and the rest of the document can still be used. Find, Redact by Pattern, Prepare Form, Recognize Text and the AI assistant go on with the other pages and say which they skipped.
- Redaction never passes a page it couldn't read: Batch's Redact Patterns and Check Redaction fail that file and save nothing, and Check Redaction says when it couldn't look at everything, and which pages.
- Batch presets, form data files and the AI assistant's answers can't stop the app with deeply nested JSON.
