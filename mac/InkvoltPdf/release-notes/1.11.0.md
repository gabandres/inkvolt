Inkvolt PDF edits whole paragraphs and translates documents with your own AI service, each paragraph kept in its place.

- Edit Text & Images edits whole paragraphs: click a paragraph and type, and its text rewraps inside its box with its own font, alignment and line spacing. What follows stays put, and you're asked before longer text would run into it. The bar above the text gives a selection another font, size, color, bold or italic.
- Translate with AI (Tools › AI Assistant): a translated copy of the pages you choose opens beside the document and scrolls with it, each paragraph in its box, tables cell by cell, bold, italic and links kept. It uses the AI endpoint you set up, shows exactly what is sent before anything is, and can be stopped any time. The document isn't changed.
- Fixed: in PDFs whose pages share their resources, a header, footer, watermark or flattening could leave other pages' text unreadable once saved.
- PDFs made to freeze the app no longer stall paragraph editing or Translate.
