Everything Inkvolt PDF for Windows does, now on the Mac too.

- Text recognition: pages photographed at an angle are straightened and a book's bent lines flattened before reading; Make Text Editable turns a scan's recognized text into real text; pages in two languages; words the reading wasn't sure of listed to check.
- Editing: a paragraph that mixes sizes or colors is edited as one; new letter pairs get the font's own kerning; a picture between a paragraph's words moves with them; Crop a picture (press C).
- Translate with AI keeps more of the page's look (underlines, colors, fonts, subscripts) and lays out Arabic and Hebrew right to left.
- Open XPS, EPUB, comic books (CBZ), FB2 and pictures as a PDF; Export Pages as Images; Convert to Word from Finder's Services menu or `--convert-to`.
- Redaction no longer lets the width of what you removed show, and Check Redaction finds black boxes drawn into a page's picture.
- Previous and Next View (⌘[ ⌘]), Keyboard Shortcuts (⌘/), Highlight Form Fields, Show in Finder, Close All, and black and white in File › Scan.
- Compare Files reads scans straightened and the right way up, and lines up a scan at another size or a quarter turn off.
- Files made to stall or crash a PDF app are refused or read only in part.
