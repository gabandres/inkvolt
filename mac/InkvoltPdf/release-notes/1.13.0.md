The fix for blank pages, and a big release of new tools.

- Fixed: a PDF opened with its password showed every page blank white in Inkvolt PDF for Mac 1.12.1 and earlier (its text could still be searched and copied). Its pages now show.
- AI tools that use your own AI assistant, each showing what is sent before it goes: Edit by Asking, Summary to Audio, Ask Across Folders and search by meaning (Inkvolt Pro), Suggest Redactions (Inkvolt Pro), and saving an AI answer as a Word document, a PDF or a note.
- Pages: Replace Pages, Resize Pages, Interleave Pages, Remove Blank Pages, Split at Blank Pages, Page Labels and Bookmarks from Headings.
- Editing and forms: turn pictures and text to any angle, rulers, guides and a grid, calculated form fields, several fields arranged at once, Find and Replace, spell check where you type, proofreading marks and Mark All Matches.
- Reading and windows: Reflow (⌘3), two documents side by side in their own windows, Copy Text from Area and Copy Area as Table, links from web and email addresses, and smaller black-and-white scans.
- Help › What's New lists every change.
