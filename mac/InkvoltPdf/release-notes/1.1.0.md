Redaction and Sanitize come to Inkvolt PDF for Mac.

- Redact areas, selected text, search matches or patterns (Social Security, phone and card numbers, emails, dates, IBANs and your own words), review the marks, then apply: what's under them is removed from the file.
- After applying, a redaction check makes sure nothing of what you redacted is left anywhere in the file; Tools › Check Redaction… checks any PDF.
- Sanitize Document removes metadata, hidden content, scripts, links, attachments and earlier versions before you share a PDF.

For Macs with Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Free software under the GNU AGPL v3; source in this repository.
