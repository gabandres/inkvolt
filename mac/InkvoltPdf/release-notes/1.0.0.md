The first release of Inkvolt PDF for Mac.

- Read PDFs in tabs, with thumbnails, the outline, find and clickable links.
- Comment, highlight and draw; fill in forms; Fill & Sign with your signature.
- Edit the text and pictures already on a page.
- Make scanned pages searchable (OCR) and export to Word, Excel or text.
- Organize pages, add bookmarks, combine files, scan to PDF, protect with a password.
- Print with pages per sheet, booklets and posters.
- Nothing you open leaves your Mac: no account, no tracking.

For Macs with Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Free software under the GNU AGPL v3; source in this repository.
