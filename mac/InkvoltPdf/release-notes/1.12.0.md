Inkvolt PDF keeps signatures checkable for years and redacts by pattern with every match listed for you to check.

- Long-term validation (Inkvolt Pro): Sign Digitally can store the proof that your certificate was good when you signed (PAdES B-LT) and add a document timestamp over it (B-LTA). Fill & Sign › Add Verification Information… does the same for files signed earlier, and Batch for a folder of them. Before anything goes online, a window lists every address that will be asked and what it gets; addresses on this Mac or your network are never contacted.
- Check Signatures Online… asks whether the certificates of a file's signatures were revoked, without changing the file. Opening or signing a file never goes online by itself, and each signature says what the file holds for checking it later.
- Certifying a form can lock all its fields, the ones you choose, or all but those (Inkvolt Pro). Timestamps now go over https only; Sectigo's free server is suggested.
- Redact by Pattern lists every match with its page and the words around it, beside the page: untick any you want to keep. Scanned pages are searched through text recognition and flagged for you to look at. Afterwards the result is read back and checked, and a report lists what was removed, masked.
- Inkvolt Pro: saved sets of patterns and phrases, your own pattern, and a set run over a folder in Batch with a removal report (CSV).
