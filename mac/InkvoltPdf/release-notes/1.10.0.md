Inkvolt PDF has the command bar of Inkvolt PDF for Windows: seven groups in the toolbar, each doing its main job in one click, with Convert › Word saving a Word document next to the PDF.

- The toolbar is the command bar: Edit, Convert, Comment, Fill & Sign, Organize, Protect and Scan & OCR, each with its label. A click on a group does its main job; its arrow lists the rest, each with a line saying what it does. Option-click asks first.
- Convert › Word saves name.docx next to the PDF in one click (never over a file), opens it, and says "Saved name.docx · Show in Finder · Change options…"; Excel, PowerPoint, text and web page too.
- Sign, Highlight, Password and Recognize Text work in one click as well. Draw is in Comment, Redact in Protect.
- A one-click conversion never writes over a file another app saved under its name meanwhile, and a name Windows can't use (such as CON) is never made.
