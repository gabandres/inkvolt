Inkvolt PDF searches whole folders of PDFs at once, compares Word, Excel and PowerPoint files and scans with PDFs, draws large plans faster, and fills more real forms, protected and damaged ones too.

- Search Folders (Edit › Find › Search Folders…, ⌥⇧⌘F): add a folder and Inkvolt PDF indexes every PDF in it on this Mac, then finds a word across all of them in a moment, with the text around each hit. A result opens at the hit.
- Compare Files compares a Word, Excel or PowerPoint file (made into a PDF with LibreOffice) or a picture of a scanned page with a PDF; a scan's words are recognized on this Mac first.
- Compare Files lists formatting changes and moved paragraphs apart from changes to the words, leaves out running headers and page numbers, and lines up two crooked scans before overlaying them. Changes read from a scan that are often misread are marked.
- Large drawings (plans and sheets with tens of thousands of lines) appear two to three times faster, drawn on all of the Mac's cores, and the next page is drawn ahead while you scroll.
- Protected forms that allow filling (many government forms, such as USCIS's) are filled and saved without asking for a password, and stay protected as they were.
- Social Security, phone and ZIP boxes with room for just the digits take them all, pasted with dashes too; the dashes appear when you leave the field.
- More real forms work: radio buttons and fields their form lists oddly, check boxes that showed no tick, a list that kept showing its old choice, a field shown on several pages.
- Damaged PDFs keep their bookmarks, fields and values when filled and saved. A damaged file says so when it opens, and a signature made before the repair is never shown as valid.
- Forms made to freeze the app open and fill in moments: fields listed on thousands of pages, a check box picture that unpacks to gigabytes, a damaged file numbered in the millions.
