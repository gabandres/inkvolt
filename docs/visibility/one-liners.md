## Launch

**X / Bluesky**

Inkvolt PDF: a free, open-source PDF editor for Windows and Mac. Edit text, fill and sign, merge pages, PDF to Word,
OCR, redact for real. No account, no ads, no telemetry. https://bermudezsystems.com/products/inkvolt/

**Mastodon** (fosstodon.org or mastodon.social; tag #OpenSource #PDF #Privacy)

Inkvolt PDF is a free, AGPL PDF editor for Windows (x64, ARM64) and Mac (Apple silicon): edit text, fill and sign,
organize and merge pages, export to Word, OCR on your own machine, and redaction that removes the content and then
checks the whole file for anything left. No account, no ads, no telemetry, nothing uploaded. Source on GitHub.
https://github.com/gabandres/inkvolt #OpenSource #PDF #Privacy

**LinkedIn**

I've released Inkvolt PDF, a free and open-source PDF editor for Windows and Mac.

It does what the free tiers of the big apps charge for: edit text on the page, fill and sign, organize and merge
pages, export to Word and Excel, OCR, create forms, compare versions, and redact. Everything runs on your own
computer; there is no account, no ads and no telemetry, and the source is on GitHub under the AGPL.

Two things I built because I needed them: a redaction check that looks for anything left of what you removed, in
metadata, comments, attachments and the file's earlier versions, not just under the black box; and "What's in This
PDF", a report of every script, link, attachment and hidden element a file carries before you share it.

Windows: Microsoft Store. Mac: notarized download or Homebrew. For a team: business licences by email.

https://bermudezsystems.com/products/inkvolt/

## Redaction

**X / Bluesky**

A black box over a name doesn't remove the name. Inkvolt PDF removes it, then checks the whole file: metadata,
comments, form fields, attachments, earlier versions. Free, open source, Windows and Mac.
https://bermudezsystems.com/products/inkvolt/redact-pdf/

## What's in This PDF

**X / Bluesky**

What's hidden in a PDF? Scripts that run when it opens, addresses it reaches, attachments, who wrote it, every earlier
version of itself. Inkvolt PDF lists all of it, free, and removes what you choose.
https://bermudezsystems.com/products/inkvolt/whats-in-a-pdf/

## PDF to Word

**X / Bluesky**

Online "PDF to Word" converters work by uploading your document. Inkvolt PDF converts on your PC, free: paragraphs,
headings, tables and forms rebuilt as Word content; scans recognized first.
https://bermudezsystems.com/products/inkvolt/pdf-to-word/

## Inkvolt Print

**X / Bluesky**

Inkvolt Print: your printer's status, ink and toner, scanning to PDF, copying and the print queue, with no printer-maker
account, no cloud and no ads. HP, Canon, Epson, Brother and any AirPrint or IPP Everywhere printer. Free, MIT.
https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9PNR76HJ62SK
